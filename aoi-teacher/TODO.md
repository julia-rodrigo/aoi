## What building the tracker will take

- A daily check-in screen: tick off each "Daily record" item, with a note, photo or
  recording where it makes sense, saved with the date.
- A timeline view per area (streaks, last done, history), so progress over weeks is visible.
- The Goal Journal as real goals with steps and dates, linked to the lessons that serve them
  (Get PPL → Checkride Prep, Travel to Japan → Japanese Pronunciation).
- Each "Learnings" area as its own learn-then-quiz subject, using the module that PPL and
  General Knowledge already share.
- Aoi's recommendations drawn from all of the above: what was skipped lately, what is close
  to done, what to watch or try next.
